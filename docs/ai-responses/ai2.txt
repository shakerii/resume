## Project case study template

1. What is it?

One or two sentences describing the product and the problem it solves.

2. My role

What you personally designed, built, or led.

3. Technical highlights

Three to five important engineering decisions or challenges.

4. Results

Real numbers, scale, time saved, or what the system enabled.

5. Technologies

A short list of relevant tools.

# 8. Your relocation job search strategy

Since you're based in Iran and want to move with your fiancé, you need to distinguish between:

1. Companies that hire remotely from Iran.

2. Companies that can sponsor a visa and relocate you.

3. Companies that hire you remotely first and may relocate later.

These are not interchangeable.

For the countries you're considering, I'd investigate roles with keywords such as:

* Senior Full-stack Engineer — Relocation.

* Senior TypeScript Engineer — Visa Sponsorship.

* AI Engineer — Relocation.

* Backend Engineer — International Candidates.

* Software Engineer — EU Blue Card.

* Highly Skilled Migrant — Netherlands.

* Skilled Worker Visa — UK.

You should also be prepared for the possibility that a company can sponsor you but has restrictions on hiring someone currently living in Iran. That is something to check with recruiters early rather than after several interviews.
